# Evangelizing and Scaling Research

## Executive Summary 
Across my career, I have joined teams undergoing significant team growth and changes. 

At **Cisco** (2022) I joined the Lifecycle Transformation Design team undergroing significant team growth (doubling from 20 to 40 pracitioners). 

At **Bright MLS** (2024) I joined a reactive, pixel-first design team seeking to shift to more proactive, research and customer-backed decision making and processes. 

And with **Capital One** (2025) I joined the matrix'd research organization supporting shifting democratization needs, spanning portfolios, and addressing emerging technology (Artifical Intelligence). 

In all three cases I was tasked with leading, fostering, and growing teams of high performing individuals while empowering non-researchers to learn the skills necessary to scale and "do more with less" 

## Approach (The What) 
While each organization was met with their own unique challenges and opportunities, my approach consistently started with fact-finding. Understanding how business, software development and definition, design, and research occur. 

From there I worked to scale democratization trainings and practices according to available tooling, organizational maturity, and ovearll appetite by my partners. 

## The Outcomes (The So What)
At Cisco, my role shifted from managing 10+ UX Designers to a team (4) of UX Researchers. Training and coaching evolved to monthly research-led lunch and learn trainings and ongoing, open-door consulting by myself and the team. Key outputs included: 
- Standardized capabilities decks 
- Repeatable trainings 
- A research community of practice elevating the team as experts 

At Bright MLS, I worked direclty with product leads, joining monthly customer calls to deploy quick, iterative research studies and to communicate to our customer base how we are enacting change with their feedback. Key outputs included: 
- Shifting existing design outputs to scalable design systems 
- Scalable, repeatable voice of the customer program 
- Positioning Design and Research as partners in the product development lifecycle 

And at Capital One, I currently work to scale democratization through facilitated and self-service training, collaborate across the business, leading to an increase of focussed research questions and actionable insights. Key outputs include: 
- Growing list of agentic tools to augment research artifact creation 
- Collaboration and scaling of democratized research training (white glove and self service modules)

## The Impact (The Now What)
While the impact varies across organization, in each case we were able to: 

- Tighten the loop (reduce time) between ideation and execution 
- Expand knowledge by increasing the number of individual compelting research 
- Increase the quality of research by providing standard tools, guardrails, and safety nets 

## Lesons Learned 
Most critically over these experiences, AI tools have become more embedded in our workflow. 

While I started as an AI skeptic, I have quickly become an advocate as I see the value of leveraging Gemini Gems for evaluative reviews. This includes gems that: 
- Evaluate survey quality
- Converse with researchers on refining quality research questions 
- Crafting research needs into standard research approach documents 
- and much more.
